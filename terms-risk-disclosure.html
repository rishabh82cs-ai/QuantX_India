Greexora Terms & Risk Disclosure

Important information about the developing Greexora experience, paper-only evaluation, financial risk, and responsible use.

Derivatives risk

Options and futures trading can cause substantial losses and may not be suitable for all users. Past performance, backtesting, simulations, and AI explanations do not guarantee future results.

Decision support only

Greexora provides general product-development information and is developing analytics, research, decision-support, and paper-evaluation experiences. It does not provide personal financial advice, guaranteed profits, or risk-free outcomes.

No current live service

Greexora does not currently offer live trading. Any future live offering would require separately approved safeguards, terms, compliance readiness, and applicable user confirmation.
