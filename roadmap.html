Six public development stages

The Greexora public development journey.

This roadmap describes customer value and broad commercial stages only. Timing and scope may change following research, testing, legal and regulatory review, and partner readiness.

01Roadmap defined

Customer and market discovery

Research the problems users face, the context they need, and the outcomes a responsible decision-support experience should deliver.

Customer value: Development begins with real user needs rather than assumptions.

02Planned

Experience design

Shape a clear and accessible experience around financial context, disciplined decisions, risk awareness, and user understanding.

Customer value: Complex information is presented in a more structured and understandable way.

03Planned

Paper-only evaluation

Evaluate intended product experiences in simulated settings without offering a live trading service or placing real capital at risk.

Customer value: Weaknesses can be identified and improved before broader exposure.

04Future development

Independent testing

Challenge usability, clarity, resilience, and risk communication through structured internal and controlled external evaluation.

Customer value: Product claims are tested against evidence before expansion.

05Future validation

Security and compliance readiness

Prepare the product, documentation, safeguards, and operating processes for formal readiness review.

Customer value: Responsible release decisions take priority over speed.

06Long-term target

Controlled paper pilot

Assess whether the evidence supports a carefully bounded paper-only pilot, subject to legal, regulatory, security, and operational readiness.

Customer value: Broader paper access occurs only when the experience and its safeguards are ready.

Development target

Paper evaluation · Q4 2026

A guided paper experience is targeted for structured evaluation. This is a development target, not a public availability guarantee.

Release principle

No public live date

Any future live capability sits outside this public roadmap and would require separate security, legal, compliance, and partner-readiness decisions.
