License-aware platform development

Third-party licenses and attributions.

This page records Greexora’s public position on third-party libraries, charting components, market-data sources, broker APIs, fonts, icons, and export tools.

TradingView-related charting policy

Greexora describes its charting as an internal professional charting system unless a formal TradingView agreement exists.

No implied affiliation

Greexora is not affiliated with TradingView unless explicitly stated under a formal agreement.

No unauthorised Advanced Charts or Trading Platform use

Greexora will not use TradingView Advanced Charts or Trading Platform libraries unless formal approval and permitted access are obtained.

Lightweight Charts attribution, if used

If Greexora uses TradingView Lightweight Charts, attribution and notices will be maintained in accordance with the applicable open-source license and NOTICE requirements.

No scraped TradingView data

Greexora must not use scraped TradingView chart data, personal subscription data, browser sessions, or user-account market data as a commercial backend data source.
