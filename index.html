From analysis to journal — with a safety gate.

Greexora is structured around a clear workflow that keeps risk controls before live execution.

1. Select instrumentExchange, segment, asset class, expiry, contract.
2. Load dataQuotes, candles, option chain, futures, news.
3. Analyse setupCandles, patterns, price action, OI, IV, Greeks.
4. Build strategyAdvisor, payoff, entry, stop, target.
5. Risk checkPosition size, margin, liquidity, event risk.
6. Paper or confirm livePaper default. Live orders require user confirmation.
