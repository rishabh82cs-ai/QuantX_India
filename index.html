Private market analytics terminal

Professional analytics for Indian index and options markets.

QuantX India is a private trading analytics terminal built for Indian index and options markets, combining broker API market data, advanced charting, option-chain intelligence, risk analytics, strategy tools and AI-assisted trade journaling.

Product overview

QuantX India is being developed as a desktop/web terminal for market analysis, charting, options intelligence and trading workflow support.

Live market charts

OHLC candlestick charts, volume, indicators, multi-timeframe analysis and chart controls.

Options analytics

Option-chain analysis, PCR, Max Pain, open-interest changes, support/resistance and market snapshots.

Strategy tools

Greeks, implied volatility, payoff analysis, strategy builder, backtesting and risk-management modules.

AI workflow

AI-assisted market explanation, trade review and structured trade journaling after each trade.

Datafeed

QuantX India uses its own Dhan API based datafeed for market data, including index candles, option-chain snapshots and analytics.
