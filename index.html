Greexora public product roadmap

From financial clarity to carefully supervised validation.

Greexora is being developed to help people approach financial and market decisions with clearer context, stronger discipline, and better risk awareness. Progress remains paper-first.

01

Discover

Research customer needs, market problems, and the outcomes a responsible decision-support experience should deliver.

02

Design

Shape a clear and accessible experience around financial context, disciplined decisions, and user understanding.

03

Evaluate

Test the experience in paper-only settings and learn from structured evidence without placing real capital at risk.

04

Prepare

Progress through independent testing, security preparation, compliance review, and controlled pilot readiness.

Development target

Paper evaluation targeted for Q4 2026.

The timing remains conditional on research, testing, security, usability, and readiness evidence.

Release principle

Paper first. No public live date.

Any future live capability sits outside this public roadmap and would require separate security, legal, compliance, and partner-readiness decisions.
