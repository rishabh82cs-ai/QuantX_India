Paper-trading-first multi-market roadmap.

Greexora expands market by market while preserving one risk, audit, reconciliation, and recovery discipline.

Foundation

Market registry, market-neutral data adapters, historical storage, sessions, currencies, fees, paper orders, positions, and execution ledger.

Market validation

India first, followed by the United States, Australia, Forex, and the UAE. Each market must pass data, paper execution, risk, reconciliation, failure, and recovery checks.

Controlled live readiness

Broker adapters remain disabled until the relevant market passes paper evidence and the user gives an explicit activation approval.

Risk and compliance notice: Greexora is an analytics, charting, strategy-planning, risk-management, paper-trading, journaling, backtesting, and broker-execution support platform. It does not guarantee profits, does not eliminate trading risk, and does not provide risk-free trading outcomes. All trading decisions, live order confirmations, and risk responsibility remain with the user. Greexora is not affiliated with TradingView unless explicitly stated under a formal agreement.
