What remains common across markets

The platform shares stable engines while market-specific adapters handle local rules.

Instrument registry

Canonical symbols, exchanges, asset classes, expiries, contract multipliers, tick sizes, currencies, and identifiers.

Data and sessions

Historical and development feeds normalised with exchange calendars, time zones, holidays, stale-data protection, and provenance.

Paper execution

Orders, partial fills, slippage, stops, targets, fees, positions, realised and unrealised P&L, and execution ledger.

Risk and controls

Loss limits, duplicate prevention, position checks, kill switch, manual confirmation, audit records, and market-level activation gates.

Reconciliation

Orders, fills, positions, cash, currencies, fees, restarts, and failure recovery checked before live readiness.

Portable experience

Consistent charting, watchlists, strategy planning, journals, reports, and review workflows across supported platforms.

Development and risk notice: Market names describe the planned Greexora rollout. Availability depends on authorised data, suitable broker APIs, technical validation, local requirements, and market-specific paper-trading evidence. Live trading is not automatically enabled.
