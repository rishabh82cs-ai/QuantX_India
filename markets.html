Multi-market · paper-first · market-neutral

Five market regions. One disciplined platform.

Shared engines, market-specific rules

The architecture reuses stable services while adapters preserve the rules and permissions of each market.

Common platform core

Historical and quote interfaces, candles, charts, strategies, paper orders, fills, positions, portfolios, audit records, and recovery.

Local market controls

Instrument identity, sessions, holidays, time zones, tick sizes, fees, currencies, leverage, margin assumptions, and permitted data paths.

One safety discipline

Paper mode, stale-data protection, duplicate prevention, loss limits, reconciliation, failure testing, restart recovery, and an emergency kill switch.

Development target

Multi-market paper release before controlled live activation.

The current roadmap targets a complete paper-trading release by December 20, 2026, followed by contingency, documentation, packaging, recovery drills, and only then gradual market-by-market live readiness.
