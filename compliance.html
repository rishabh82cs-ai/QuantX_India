Greexora Compliance

This page defines the public compliance position for Greexora's research, analytics, market-data, risk-communication, and paper-only product-development direction.

TradingView-safe charting position

Greexora should describe its charting as an internal professional charting system unless a formal TradingView agreement exists.

No implied affiliation

Greexora is not affiliated with TradingView unless explicitly stated under a formal agreement.

No unauthorised claims

Do not use phrases such as TradingView-powered, official TradingView charts, or TradingView integrated unless approval is obtained.

Approved library use only

Any open-source charting component must be used according to its license and attribution requirements.

Advanced libraries only after approval

TradingView Advanced Charts or Trading Platform libraries must only be used after formal approval and under permitted terms.

Market data and broker API policy

Greexora must use approved broker APIs, licensed market-data vendors, exchange-authorised sources, or other legally permitted data sources.

Permitted data path

Broker API, approved exchange feed, authorised vendor, or legally permitted historical/source data.

Not permitted

No scraped TradingView data, no personal TradingView subscription data, and no use of browser sessions as backend data sources.

Audit requirement

Maintain data-source records, API permissions, vendor licenses, and rate-limit notes.

Multi-market rollout principles

Each market is assessed independently. Development plans for one region do not promise data, product, partner, or service availability in another.

Local readiness review

Market context, data permissions, product scope, partner availability, and local requirements are reviewed region by region.

Paper evaluation

Intended product experiences are evaluated in simulated settings before any broader paper pilot is considered.

Separate future decision

No live service is currently offered or dated. Any future proposal would require a separate legal, regulatory, security, and partner-readiness decision.

Risk communication and paper-only safeguards

Greexora is being developed as decision support and must not be presented as personal financial advice or a guaranteed-profit system.

Paper-only development

Current public evaluation is limited to simulated and paper experiences. No live trading service is currently offered.

Future safeguards

If any future live offering is considered, its terms, safeguards, risk acknowledgement, and applicable user confirmation would be defined before release.

No misleading claims

Avoid guaranteed, sure-shot, fixed profit, 100% accuracy, risk-free, or automatic profit claims.
