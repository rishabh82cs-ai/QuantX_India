Demo modules shown

The public demo represents the main planned modules without exposing broker credentials, real order routing, or restricted third-party assets.

Professional charting

Internal OHLC charting with hollow candles, volume, overlays, levels, pattern labels, and time controls.

Pattern engine

Classical candlestick pattern recognition with bullish, bearish, reversal, continuation, and indecision labels.

Price action

Trend, swing structure, support/resistance, breakout, retest, pullback, and supply/demand zones.

Options intelligence

Option chain, PCR, Max Pain, OI change, IV, Greeks, call/put pressure zones, and heatmaps.

Futures analytics

Futures price, OI, volume, basis, rollover, spot-vs-futures, and futures trade planning.

Strategy advisor

Risk-aware strategy category suggestions for bullish, bearish, range, breakout, reversal, hedged, or no-trade conditions.

Order workflow

Pre-filled paper order ticket with entry, stop, target, trailing stop, and reward:risk controls.

AI trade journal

Post-trade explanation, mistake review, lessons, EOD review, and weekly performance insights.

Compliance-friendly demo language

No TradingView claim

Greexora is presented as an internal professional charting system unless formal third-party approval exists.

No live execution

Public demo is paper-only. Live trading is disabled and would require user confirmation in production.

No profit guarantee

Greexora supports analysis, planning, risk control, journaling, and education. It does not guarantee outcomes.
