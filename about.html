What Greexora is

A disciplined financial and market decision-support project.

Greexora is being developed to help users organise relevant financial and market context, evaluate decisions more carefully, practise in paper-only settings, and learn from structured review.

India-first

The project began with Indian-market needs and remains focused on responsible, locally aware product research and paper evaluation.

Paper-first

Current development and public evaluation are simulated and paper-only. Greexora does not currently offer a live trading service.

Risk-aware

The public direction emphasises context, discipline, uncertainty, user understanding, and responsible testing—not guaranteed outcomes.

Name clarification

Greexora is the official spelling and primary brand.

QuantX India was the former project name used on this domain. Greexora is independent and is not affiliated with other companies, products, or websites using QuantX or similar names. This clarification helps customers and search engines distinguish the Greexora project from unrelated organisations.
